Training NN Models

Steps to Training the Model: Follow the same steps for future NNs 

1. Make an instance of the model/class
2. Define the loss function.
    We will use Cross Entropy Loss for Classification. - loss_function(model_prediction, labels) 
        - labels is 0s or 1s on what is the percentage for the true answer
    MSE would be for predicting a specific number // price of an uber or how tall in the future
3. Optimizer = torch.optim.Adam(model.parameters()): This will perform the Gradient Decent (Adam)
    - parameters: we are passing in the weights 
4. Define the epochs = how many times are we passing/iterating to train the model. 
    - We do not want too many epochs vs too little for over/underfitting 
5. train_dataloader: iterator that will be used to train based on this dataset (tuples)

Training Body:
6. Call the forward method: model_prediction = model(images)
7. We want to recalculate the derivatives by doing: optimizer.zero_grad()
8. Calculating the loss of the trained data set: loss = loss_function(model_prediction, labels) 
9. Calcualte every derivative necessary for gradient decent: 
    - Based on the NN size, might have a lot to calculate
    - loss.backward(): store the necessary derivatives: derivatives for each weight for gradient descent is calculated
10. Getting the new wights and going a step in the right direction for the right weights:
    - optimizer.step(): new_w = old_w - derivative * learning_rate 



Code:

model = DigitReconition()

loss_function = nn.CrossEntropyLoss()
optimizer = torch.optim.Adam(model.parameters())

epochs = 5

for epoch in range(epochs):
    for images, labels in train_dataloader:
        images = images.view(images.shape[0], 784)
        
        # Training Body
        
        model_prediction = model(images)
        optimizer.zero_grad()
        loss = loss_function(model_prediction, labels)
        loss.backward()
        optimizer.step()
